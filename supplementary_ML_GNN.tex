This document holds the material that supports the manuscript without being part of its argument: the architecture
chosen by the inner loop in every outer fold, and the detail of the screening, docking and ADMET stage, whose results
are hypotheses for experimental testing rather than conclusions of the benchmark. Every value is generated by the
analysis pipeline and read from the same files as the manuscript.
