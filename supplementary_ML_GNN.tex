This document holds the material that supports the manuscript without being part of its argument: the architecture
chosen by the inner loop in every outer fold, and the detail of the screening, docking and ADMET stage, whose results
are hypotheses for experimental testing rather than conclusions of the benchmark. Every value is generated by the
analysis pipeline and read from the same files as the manuscript.

\section*{Benchmark detail}

\begin{figure}[htbp]
	\centering
	\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figures/S1_nested_cv_selection_full_heatmap.png}
	\caption{Stacking ensemble selected by the inner loop in each of the \TotalOuterFolds\ outer folds, for each molecular representation. Cell values are the number of outer folds in which that exact combination of base learners (RF, random forest; ET, extra trees; LGBM, LightGBM; XGB, XGBoost; SVM, support vector regression; kNN, $k$-nearest neighbors) was chosen. No combination was chosen consistently, which shows that the inner search does not converge on a single architecture.}
	\label{fig:selection_heatmap}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[htbp]
	\centering
	\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figures/01_nested_cv_performance_summary.png}
	\caption{Per-fold performance of the four representations over the \TotalOuterFolds\ outer folds. (A) $R^2$ of each fold (faint points), with the mean and its 95\% confidence interval; (B) the same for the mean absolute error. The vertical lines mark the D-MPNN of this study and the three values reported by the original study, which come from a single hold-out split of a pipeline with the leaks described in the manuscript and are therefore shown for reference only, not as a paired comparison.}
	\label{fig:performance_summary}
\end{figure}
